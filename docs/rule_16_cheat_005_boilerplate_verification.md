# CHEAT-005: Boilerplate Verification Claims

## What is it?
**CHEAT-005 (Boilerplate verification claims)** is defined in `AGENTS.md` (under Rule 16: Anti-cheat manifesto) as the practice of leaving "repeated comments asserting verification without a linked proof or receipt."

## Why is it prohibited?
In the BCINR framework, human or agent assertions are not accepted as evidence of correctness. The architecture demands a strict **deterministic computational substrate** governed by mathematical contracts, structured laws, and reproducible artifacts. 

Specifically, this cheat is prohibited because:
1. **Assertions are not Proofs**: The constitution explicitly states that the repository does not accept implementations that merely claim or appear to be correct. Verification cannot be asserted through prose; it must be backed by mechanical evidence.
2. **Mandatory Mechanical Artifacts**: Any claim of verification must be backed by a verifiable artifact, such as a linked mathematical proof, a bit-vector solver certificate, a Hoare contract, or a cryptographic receipt generated by the pipeline.
3. **Rejection of Consensus as Evidence**: As reinforced by Rule 27 ("No self-certification"), "Agent agreement is not evidence. Five agents repeating the same claim is still one unsupported claim." Boilerplate comments attempt to bypass the strict structural audits (enforced by `@turing_machine`) and mathematical laws (owned by `@hoare_oracle`) by substituting textual claims for rigorous, reproducible proof.
