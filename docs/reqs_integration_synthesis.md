### 2. Cross-Repository Boundaries (`bcinr` / `praxis` / `mfact`)

With `bcinr` serving as the deterministic substrate ($CC=1$, branchless logic, zero allocation on the hot path), the boundaries to `praxis` (planning and action execution) and `mfact` (higher-order reasoning and factories) must be airtight and semantically rigorous.

**Handling `Pddl8Error::PlanningFailed` Exhaustiveness in `praxis`**
- **The Issue:** `praxis` currently interacts with `bcinr`'s PDDL solvers. When a plan fails (`Pddl8Error::PlanningFailed`), the nature of this failure is often ambiguous. It is critical to distinguish whether the search space exhausted natively (a provably unsolvable domain) or whether it hit an artificial resource limit (timeout, memory, depth bound).
- **Requirement:** `bcinr-pddl` must emit a strongly-typed failure variant detailing the exact exhaustion proof.
  - Modify `Pddl8Error::PlanningFailed` to include a `FailureReason` enum: `StateSpaceExhausted` (perfect proof), `CapacityExceeded`, `Timeout`, etc.
  - **`praxis` Integration:** `praxis` must match exhaustively on this error. 
    - If `StateSpaceExhausted`, `praxis` can safely conclude the goal is impossible under current facts and fail fast.
    - If `Timeout` or `CapacityExceeded`, `praxis` MUST either retry with expanded bounds, or escalate the fault to `mfact` for strategic replanning (e.g., relaxing constraints, restructuring the task, or requesting new sub-capabilities).

**Inter-Repo API Stability & Determinism**
- **`bcinr` to `mfact`:** `mfact` relies on the exact concurrency complexes generated by `bcinr` to reason about throughput and layout. `bcinr-powl` MUST NOT leak legacy 32-byte op abstractions to `mfact`. `CompiledPowlV2` is the only authorized IPC data structure for topology exchange across this boundary.
- **`mfact` to `praxis`:** When `mfact` synthesizes new behaviors, it injects them into `praxis`. The `praxis` PDDL state updates must be deterministically serialized and fed back into `bcinr`'s autonomic loop without introducing Von Neumann branching, preserving the substrate's integrity score (SIS = 100).
